**Push scope:** new enquiries can send an opt-in alert to every registered staff device and open the matching enquiry. New local workflow triggers now queue alerts for quote, build, appointment, invoice and recorded-payment changes. They omit customer and financial values, skip the staff member who made the change, open the relevant app section and use the same retry worker. A scheduled worker retries failed email/push delivery with backoff; partial delivery to a staff member's active devices remains retryable, and expired device endpoints are removed without falsely reporting a zero-device delivery as sent. ## Security and operating rules

- Never put a service-role key, database password, Resend key, VAPID private key, push dispatch token or other secret in Git, browser config or the public Vercel build. Only the VAPID public key belongs in the staff browser config.
- Keep the retry worker token in Supabase Function Secrets and Supabase Vault; the cron SQL retrieves it from Vault when dispatching, without embedding its value in the job definition.
- The Operations app shell and public anon key can be fetched by anyone. Never place staff records or private credentials in its static files; Supabase Auth and RLS remain the authorization boundary for every business record.
- Public requests go through the validated Edge Function. Do not add anonymous direct writes to private tables.
- The function rejects missing and unapproved browser origins, but `Origin` can be forged by non-browser clients. Its HMAC-based edge-IP limiter is a separate control and must be live, tested and disclosed in the site's privacy notice before routine public traffic.
- Store customer images privately and grant each staff user only the access needed. Upload and download flows are not implemented yet.
- Keep each enquiry's status, preferred dates and confirmed bookings factually distinct. A confirmed status alone is not a calendar reservation.
- Test with fictional records until the specific project and exact customer data flow have been authorised.
- Web Push permission is requested only after a signed-in staff member presses the opt-in button. Push delivery remains best-effort; the saved enquiry and in-app list are the source of truth.
