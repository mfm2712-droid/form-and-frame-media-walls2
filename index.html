A-04 / How a wall gets built

Five steps, one team, no subcontractors.

01

Survey

Laser measure, check the wall for square, mark power, aerial and any pipework to move.

02

Drawing

A scaled elevation with the full spec. You sign it off before anything is ordered.

03

Workshop

Carcasses and veneered faces built off-site in Slough, dry-assembled and checked.

04

Install

Two days on site. Dust sheets, tidy cuts, cable runs brushed and hidden.

05

Handover

Snag the doors, level the shelves, pair the fire remote, walk you through it.
